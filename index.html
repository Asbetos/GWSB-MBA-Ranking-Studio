How the workspace composes

In Direct mode the simulation reads the nine methodology indicators straight from the sliders. In Indirect mode the operational levers feed the eight Core Feature Models (RidgeCV / ElasticNetCV pipelines fitted per indicator), whose predictions populate the direct sliders read-only and then feed the same simulation. Only one set is interactive at a time — switch modes to compare lever pathways without their values colliding. The active scoring engine then runs a residual-anchored Monte Carlo (10,000 draws · rank-tier-calibrated Gaussian noise on competitor scores), so the rank median and 90% interval above always correspond to the engine selected at top.

Engine diagnostics & feature signals

Side-by-side fit statistics for the five scoring engines, all trained on the stacked 2024 + 2025 panel (n ≈ 243 school-years × 9 indicators) and evaluated via 5-fold cross-validation against the published US News Overall Score. The ElasticNet engine here is the same Direct Score Model that powers the GWSB Ranking Predictor (v2) — use this tab to read v2's coefficients in context, and to triangulate whether the v2 linear fit holds up against the four non-linear alternatives before committing to a recommendation.

Per-indicator signal

Signed coefficients (ElasticNet, Stacked meta-learner) reveal direction; tree-based importances and MLP weight magnitudes are unsigned and only express relative leverage. Compare across engines to identify indicators whose effect direction is method-robust vs. method-dependent.

03 Per-School Comparison

Cohort disagreement · published vs. five engines

Engine-by-engine rank reconciliation against the published US News ranking for the 2025 cohort. Δrank = (model rank − published rank); positive values mean the engine placed the school worse than US News did. Sort by |Δ| to surface the schools where a given engine systematically dissents from the published methodology — these are the cells worth opening when validating a v2 recommendation, refining a Core Feature Model, or sourcing rebuttal data for indicator submissions.
